Who She Is

A trusted name in women's health.

Dr. Shannon Francesca Fernandes is a well-known Obstetrician & Gynaecologist with 16 years of experience. She has practised as an expert across different cities of India and within many reputed hospitals, contributing to the management of numerous complex medical cases.

She is known for her attention to accurate diagnosis and for treating every patient with genuine empathy — believing that how care is delivered matters as much as the care itself. She has conducted over 3,000 deliveries and performed more than 2,000 gynaecological surgeries.
